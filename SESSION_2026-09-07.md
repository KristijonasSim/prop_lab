## 2. The two fixes, and why they existed at all

### Fix 1 — the volatility guard was numerically wrong

`vwstd` is computed as `sqrt(p2v/v − vwap²)`. That is a difference of two
nearly-equal accumulated sums, which is the classic catastrophic-cancellation
shape. Its floating-point error floor is `price · sqrt(eps)` — about **3e-5 on
gold**, which is exactly the 3.1e-5 the cross-check observed.

A session made entirely of padded weekend bars has a **true** volume-weighted
sigma of exactly zero. The kernel's only guard was `if sd <= 0.0: skip`, and
3.1e-5 sails through it. **So the kernel was building trade bands out of rounding
noise, in a market that was closed.**

The guard is now `sd <= vwap * SD_EPS_FRAC` with `SD_EPS_FRAC = 1e-6` —
about 70x above the cancellation floor and about 2,000x below the smallest real
band on gold. `strategies/vwap/engine.py`.

### Fix 2 — the kernel traded bars where the market was shut

Dukascopy pads the closed FX weekend with synthetic bars: **zero volume,
O=H=L=C at the last traded price.** Measured this session:

| timeframe | bars | zero-volume | share |
|---|---|---|---|
| XAUUSD 5m | 270,144 | 57,975 | **21.46%** |
| XAUUSD 15m | 90,048 | 19,320 | 21.46% |
| XAUUSD 1h | 22,512 | 4,819 | 21.41% |
| XAUUSD 4h | 5,628 | 843 | 14.98% |
| BTCUSDT 1h | 26,304 | **0** | 0% |

Both kernels now take a `live` array and refuse to **decide** on such a bar and
refuse to **fill** on one. Crypto is unaffected — it has none.

## 3. The result on H-002 (VWAP, gold)

`strategies/vwap/stage20_deadfix.py`. The pre-fix walk-forward was kept and the
post-fix one written beside it, so the comparison is measured, not assumed.

### The signal survives, and the median cell got BETTER

| | before | after |
|---|---|---|
| cells clearing PF 1.20 at 2x measured cost | 15 / 20 | **15 / 20** |
| paired null, identical procedure | 1 / 20 | 2 / 20 |
| median cell PF@2x | 1.395 | **1.485** |
| gold walk-forward trades | 18,119 | 17,432 (−3.8%) |

That is the right shape for this fix. If the dead bars had been carrying the
edge, removing them would have collapsed the real side toward the null. They were
carrying noise.

### The pace headline does NOT survive

| book | expected days, two-step |
|---|---|
| 5m + 4h equal — the old board book, re-scored | 100.2 → **185.8** |
| the 78-combination search re-run on fixed data | **143.6** |

**Why a 3.8% trade cut nearly doubled the day count.** The old book's peak
drawdown was **8.00R against an 8.00R cap — exactly on the line.** The fix took
it to 8.60R, `riskladder.pick` drops a risk rung, and the day count roughly
doubles. **The 100-day number was never robust**; any change of that size in
either direction would have moved it as much. This is the same cap that T3 item 3
found binds before the daily-loss limit.

### The new book

`5m + 30m + 1h + 4h`, weighted by **signal-to-cost** — total R over that leg's own
max drawdown, normalised. That is exactly the weighting H-012 said was missing
when a wider book made things slower, and it now wins on its own without being
forced.

PF 2.016 · PF@2x 1.850 · **4.41 trades/day** · maxDD 7.52R · R/day +0.156 ·
**143.6 expected days two-step, 55.9 one-step.**

**Control:** 3 of 78 real books come in under 150 expected days against **0 of
78** for the null, and the null's fastest book needs 474.7 days.

## 6. `core/verify_board.py` was auditing a book that died three weeks ago

Found while checking the new numbers. `HANDOFF.md` says to hand this file to
anyone who wants to audit the project. It was verifying **BTCUSDT 4h + ETHUSDT
1h/30m + SOLUSDT 4h + XAUUSD 5m** — the book of 2026-09-01. **Four of those five
legs died on 2026-09-06** when the look-aheads came out, and the file predates
this session's fix entirely.

An audit tool that verifies a book the board no longer carries is worse than no
audit tool. Rewritten to read the same walk-forward the board reads. It now
reproduces PF 2.016, maxDD 7.518R, R/day +0.15627 from first principles with no
imports from `strategies/` or `core/`.

One bug was found in the rewrite and is worth recording because it is the same
class as the ones this session fixed: `max_drawdown_R` returns a **negative**
number, so `max(dd, 1e-9)` silently returned `1e-9` for every leg and the
signal-to-cost weights degenerated. Caught because the tool disagreed with the
board. **That is the audit tool doing its job on its first run.**

## 7. The conversation that matters more than the numbers

Kris pushed back on the project itself, and he was right to. His words, roughly:
*"one day I log in there is a bug, other day a missing hypothesis, other day it
seems we don't have a test suite, other day everything works and we have a
strategy with 8.9 score, other day it was a bug and all our hypotheses are
shit."*

### What was established

**There is no test suite.** Verified, not assumed:

| exists | covers |
|---|---|
| `smoke_test.py` | environment only — do imports work |
| `strategies/ribbon/test_parity.py` | ribbon's **indicators** only |
| pytest / hypothesis / CI | **none installed, none configured** |

**The VWAP kernel — which produced every board number this project has ever
published — has zero tests.**

**How every bug was actually found:**

| bug | found by |
|---|---|
| three look-aheads (killed all crypto) | reading the code by hand |
| sigma noise on closed markets | porting the kernel to a second engine |
| trading dead weekend bars | chasing that port disagreement |
| negative-drawdown weighting bug | the rewritten audit tool disagreeing |

All of them by cross-checks and effort. **None by a safety net.**

### The diagnosis, stated plainly

Kris asked whether a database, a VM, or an off-the-shelf test suite from GitHub
would fix this. The honest answers:

* **A database would not have caught a single one of these bugs.** They were all
  in the numba kernel. Storage does not validate arithmetic. This is a
  correctness problem, not an infrastructure problem.
* **An off-the-shelf suite tests someone else's code.** The *framework* is worth
  buying — `pytest`, and `hypothesis` to generate adversarial inputs instead of
  guessing them. The *invariants* cannot be bought: "a trade must never use a bar
  that has not closed" is a fact about our strategy that no package knows.
* **The strongest off-the-shelf method is the one that already worked** —
  NautilusTrader as a second engine. That is what caught the kernel flaw. The gap
  is that it was run once, by hand, for one strategy.

**The real root cause: results are published before they are verified.** The
pipeline is kernel → sweep → walk-forward → board, and the null test, the second
engine and the look-ahead audit happen *afterwards*, ad hoc, when someone
remembers. That is precisely why 8.9 becomes "it was a bug". **H-009 reached 8.9
without ever being checked by a second engine, and nothing in the system stopped
it.**

### Two fair corrections made to Kris's framing

* The "missing hypotheses" were **not** a bug — that was his own prune call at
  board score ≥ 5.0 (commit `5438f7c`), and every file is recoverable from git.
* **Markdown is not the mess.** Results already live in `board.json` and parquet;
  the `.md` files are the reasoning, which is the correct place for it. The mess
  is that there are **five overlapping entry-point documents** — addressed in
  this session's cleanup.

### What was agreed

Items **1, 2 and 3** below, in that order, and **no new hypotheses until they
are in.** Item 4 (one `Strategy` interface, kernels as a tested library) is a
refactor, not a fix, and comes after.
