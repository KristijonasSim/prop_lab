## Mechanism

BTC has no cash flow, so it behaves like a long-duration asset discounted at the real rate. A real yield near its annual high tightens the liquidity that funds marginal speculative buyers. The loser is the late leveraged long who is financing at a higher hurdle, so the marginal seller is a de-risking allocator. This is the macro-liquidity channel, and it has not been paired with a crypto market here.

Source: FRED. US 10-year real yield (TIPS).
