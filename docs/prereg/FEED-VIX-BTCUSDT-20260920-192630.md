## Mechanism

A VIX at the top of its one-year range coincides with forced deleveraging, where margin calls force leveraged holders to sell liquid risk assets, including crypto perps, regardless of value. Once the forced selling is done, the price sits below where willing holders would clear it. The loser is the forced seller, and the winner is whoever supplies liquidity at the extreme. Direction +1 is a contrarian bet that the ten-day forward return after a stress peak is positive.

Source: CBOE. CBOE equity volatility index.
