## Mechanism

This is the same real-rate differential channel as EURUSD, tested on a second USD-quoted pair whose funding profile differs, because the UK runs a larger current-account deficit. A high real US yield draws in hedged carry buyers of dollars. The loser is the sterling holder who is dependent on external funding and is repriced against the rising US real rate. Treat it as a robustness check of the EURUSD cell, not as independent evidence.

Source: FRED. US 10-year real yield (TIPS).
