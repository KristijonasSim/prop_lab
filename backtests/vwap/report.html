WALK-FORWARD

The only numbers with no hindsight in them

Quarterly folds: train on the trailing twelve months, trade the next three, roll. The configuration and the filter are chosen on the train slice alone and re-chosen every fold, so nothing on this page below saw the quarter it traded. Two choices had to be made about how to pick inside a fold — a trade-count floor of 30 or 100, and the single best config or the top ten equally weighted — so all four run and all four are reported. A real edge should not care much which is used.

PORTFOLIO

Combining markets to pass faster

255 books, 9 legs, one per market

Independent strategies add trades without adding proportional drawdown, so the same risk budget resolves sooner. Whether that works depends entirely on the correlation between the daily P&L series — measured below, not assumed. Each row is the best book at that leg count, sized so peak drawdown still fits inside the 8% cap.

Why it works: the legs barely touch each other

Pairwise correlation of daily returns. Anything under about 0.2 is close enough to independent that the drawdowns do not stack.

Does not survive the walk-forward. Rebuilt from walk-forward trades on the window every leg shares, the four-leg book scores 1.018–1.222 against 1.435 for the two legs that actually hold. The 0.023 correlation is real; correlation between legs is worthless when two of the legs have no current edge.
01

The fill test

band families only

A band strategy is naturally traded with a resting limit order at the band. A backtest that assumes a wick touch fills you is assuming queue priority it cannot verify — the old repo has a strategy that backtested at PF 3.0 and traded live at 0.7 for exactly this reason. So every band config is run twice: a limit at the band, and a close beyond it with entry at the next open.
