So when do we know a hypothesis is dead?

Plenty of people trade ORB. Here is the checklist this desk uses, and honestly where ORB sits on it.

Six of seven say dead. The second one says something more useful: the test never contained the mechanism. The published edge is not "breakouts work" — it is picking the 20 stocks out of 7,000 with abnormal opening volume, every day, and trading their opening range. The same paper's unfiltered version scored Sharpe 0.48, and that unfiltered version is what a single-symbol sweep like this one actually resembles.
