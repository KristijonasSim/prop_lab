Risk per trade: the speed/survival trade-off

Where the score comes from

Speed is weighted heaviest because a strategy that cannot resolve inside the phase window cannot be traded now, whatever else it does. Speed is measured as expected days per funded account — median days to pass divided by the pass rate — so an idea that blows up two accounts to get one through does not read as fast. Every day count on this page is the two-step evaluation the project targets (8% then 5% on cTrader, decided 2026-09-01). The second step is not half the work of the first: it is a second chance to breach, and the drawdown is paid twice.
