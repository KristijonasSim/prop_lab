Which firm can actually run our bot?

Ranked by expected days to a funded account for H-009, simulated under each firm's own rules at the largest risk their drawdown cap allows — not by their marketing. Firms that ban automation are listed last whatever their numbers say.

Re-check

This asks Claude from memory. It cannot browse. Treat anything it returns as a prompt to go and verify on the firm's own site, never as a new fact. Prices and rules change monthly and a model's recollection of them is exactly the kind of number this project does not accept without a source.

Days and pass rates are simulated on H-009's walk-forward trades from 2024-09 onward: a fresh account opened every trading day, fixed risk, real breaches, no size-shrinking risk manager. Expected days is median days to pass divided by the pass rate, so an idea that kills accounts on the way does not read as fast. Crypto-only firms are run on the four-leg book, which draws down 4.23R against the five-leg book's 2.82R — that, not their rules, is what makes them slow. Every cost assumption is Binance perpetual fees; a CFD firm's spreads will be wider and that has not been measured. Edit any rule below and the whole row re-simulates.
