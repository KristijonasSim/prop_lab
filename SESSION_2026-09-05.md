## THE IMPORTANT PART — bug #3 hits the board

`strategies/vwap/engine.py` decided on closed bar `i`, filled at open of `i+1`,
then read filters at `entry_i` (= `i+1`):

```
rvol[entry_i]  = volume[i+1] / mean(prior 1920)   <- bar i+1's OWN volume
atr[entry_i]   = EWMA incl. bar i+1's high/low    <- stop sizing
ema[entry_i]   = EWMA incl. close[i+1]
```

None of that exists when the order is placed. `atr_rank` was fine (has `.shift(1)`).

**Impact — board's most-selected BTCUSDT 4h config, PF at 2x cost:**

| filter | as-is | fixed |
|---|---|---|
| rvol>1.5 | 1.381 | 0.607 |
| rvol>2.0 | 2.120 | 0.657 |
| rvol>2.5 | 2.765 | 0.627 |

- Same collapse at 1h and 30m.
- Median participation lift **+0.869 -> +0.090**.
- **This is where CLAUDE.md's "participation filters are the only family that
  lifts it" finding came from.** A tighter threshold selects harder for bars
  that turned out busy. Busy bars are the ones that moved.

**Blast radius: 85.7% of the 2,584 board folds.**
- 74.5% use `min_rvol`, 32.7% use ATR stops.
- H-009 crypto legs: BTC 1h/30m **100%**, BTC 4h 97%, ETH 1h/30m **100%**, SOL 92-95%.

Status: **kernel fixed** (reads bar `i` now). Evidence in
`strategies/vwap/stage16_filterlook.py` + `backtests/queue/stage16_filterlook.csv`.

Caveat: collapse measured on BTC only. ETH/SOL don't load through `load_tf`.

### The other two bugs (found earlier, both fixed)

- **`fill_mode=0` same-bar look-ahead.** Booked fill on bar `i` using `h[i]`/`l[i]`,
  never advanced `entry_i`. Gave PF 27-95, Sharpe 15.5, max DD 1.17R over 1,660
  trades. Did NOT touch the board (all board folds are `fill_mode=1`).
- Consequence: the 61 cells in `stage1_grid.parquet` with >=5 trades/day and
  PF>=1.2 are **all** `fill_mode=0` fade configs. They were artifacts. **The
  fast-book shape does not exist in 1.4M+ configs tested here.**
